💗 Meri Mamma ke naam

Mamma, main aapse ek baat kehna chahta hoon, jo shayad saamne se theek se nahi keh paata.

Aapke behaviour ka mere din par asar padta hai. Aapse achhi baat ho jaaye toh mera din achha lagta hai, aur jab hum theek se baat nahi kar paate, toh main udaas ho jaata hoon.

Main aapse ye nahi keh raha ki aap mujhse din bhar baat karo. Main bas itna chahta hoon ki jab aapko waqt mile, toh mujhe bhi apna samjho. Kabhi khud se poochh lo ki main kaisa hoon, mera din kaisa gaya, main kya kar raha hoon. Kyunki main bhi aapse pyaar karta hoon, aur meri bhi feelings hain.

Ho sakta hai main kabhi aapko theek se samajh nahi paata, aur ho sakta hai aap bhi mujhe na samajh paati ho. Lekin agar humein zindagi ek-doosre ke saath bitani hai, toh humein ek-doosre ko samajhne ki koshish toh karni padegi na? Koi kisi ko ek din mein poori tarah nahi samajh sakta, lekin saath rehkar ek-doosre ko jaan zaroor sakta hai.

Mamma, main aapse gussa nahi hoon. Main bas kabhi-kabhi chahta hoon ki aap meri khamoshi ke peeche ki feelings ko bhi samjhein. Main har baar shikayat nahi karna chahta, bas chahta hoon ki hum dono ki taraf se pyaar aur koshish ho.

Main aapse pyaar karta hoon. Lekin main ye bhi chahta hoon ki hum dono ki feelings ko samajhne ki jagah ho. Main aapko majboor nahi karna chahta, bas apne dil ki baat aap tak pahunchana chahta hoon.

Agar kabhi main kam baat karne lagoon, toh iska matlab ye zaroori nahi ki mera pyaar khatam ho gaya. Kabhi-kabhi mujhe apne mann ko sambhalne ke liye thoda waqt chahiye hota hai.

Aap mere liye important ho, Mamma. Bas kabhi-kabhi main bhi aapki life mein important mehsoos karna chahta hoon. ❤️
